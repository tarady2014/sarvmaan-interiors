## Risk Mitigation: Avoid These Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake 1: Lying About Project Experience
```
DON'T: "We've completed 20 projects in Kohinoor Famville"
(If you haven't actually done this)

DO: "We specialize in serving Kohinoor Famville residents"
(Truthful, and builds authority over time)
```

### ❌ Mistake 2: Duplicate Content (Bad for SEO)
```
DON'T: Copy/paste same content for all 256 society pages
REASON: Google penalizes duplicate content

DO: Use AI to generate unique variations with:
- Different sentence structures
- Different examples/analogies
- Different emphasis on benefits
- Society-specific details
```

### ❌ Mistake 3: No Call-to-Action
```
DON'T: Create pages with no way to contact you
DO: Include WhatsApp, email, consultation link on EVERY page
```

### ❌ Mistake 4: Ignoring Society-Specific Details
```
DON'T: Generic content that could apply to ANY city
DO: Mention:
- [Area]'s specific architectural style
- [Society]'s typical apartment sizes
- [Region]'s climate considerations
- Local construction norms
```

### ❌ Mistake 5: Expecting Instant Rankings
```
DON'T: Expect all 256 pages to rank immediately
REALITY: 
- Week 1-2: Pages get indexed

- Month 3-6: Medium keywords rank (30-50 keywords)
```
